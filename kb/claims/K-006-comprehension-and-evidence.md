# Understanding and evidence support need separate checks

The reviewed studies measure different outcomes: delayed conceptual learning, assisted task performance and reliance on advice. They do not establish that an auditor who recalls an explanation has verified its implementation claims or established security.

The proposed harness consequence is to evaluate source support and auditor understanding separately. Walkthroughs should derive from the same revision-bound records as agent contexts. Optional prediction or reconstruction may reveal a misunderstanding, including an agent's error; it cannot substitute for semantic review, executable evidence or human finding adjudication.

**Limits:** these are classroom and controlled-task studies, not expert smart-contract audits. The workflow remains a recommendation with untested domain transfer. Mandatory interaction can add effort and reduce preference.

**Next check:** compare source-supported model quality, causal/source-location tasks, human effort and preference in the proposed comprehension pilot; retain unknown outcomes.
