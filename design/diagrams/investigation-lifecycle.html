Foundation · DESIGNED vs IMPLEMENTED · DEC-006

Build DESIGNED

Documentation first, developed with auditor input and applicable evidence. Candidate reconstructions stay attributed and provisional.

Trace IMPLEMENTED

Code, configuration and execution evidence establish actual behavior, including exceptions and failure paths.

Compare at every lens

Purpose → flows / states → stores → transitions. Map corresponding meaning, then retain discrepancies and comparison gaps.

Implementation deviations + design weaknesses + unresolved comparisons → proposed investigation questions
Broad orientation + review of relevant flow; other gaps remain visible
01 · Main work path

1 · Proposed

A question links its origin, scope, model revisions and relevant comparison or design-risk concern.

2 · Queued

Human batch authorization and a resource reservation are current.

3 · Running

The controller grants an attempt with frozen inputs and limited capabilities.

4 · Ready for review

The controller checks packet completeness. The auditor assesses its meaning.

Auditor reviews evidence and records a disposition
02 · Human dispositions

Accepted

Evidence policy is satisfied: normally clean replay; justified alternative evidence requires explicit auditor approval.

Rejected

Record the refutation, its premises, revision and limits.

Disputed or deferred

The concern remains unresolved and appears in closure review.

03 · Exceptions and return paths

Execution stops early

Blocked, failed or cancelled work retains its reason and partial evidence. The investigation remains unresolved.

More work is needed

The auditor requests another check. Return to queued work only under current authorization.

Blocker resolved + authorization current → queued · Human requests more work → queued
04 · Relevant change

Mark applicability stale

Accepted, rejected and unresolved dispositions retain their history. A change to DESIGNED, IMPLEMENTED or a shared premise triggers comparison and impact review; reauthorization returns work to the queue.

Changed premise → stale applicability → impact review + reauthorization → queued
Keep four fields separate: work state, human disposition, evidence quality and freshness. Discrepancies are leads, not automatic findings. Model agreement does not establish design safety. Deferred concerns remain visible.
